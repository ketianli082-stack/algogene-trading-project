# EXP-007 — V1.2 hourly ATR position sizing

Role: Development. Requested 2021-01 through 2023-12, BTCUSD, dataset=60, hourly history and hourly decisions. Results: NOT RUN at registration; read the automatically archived platform bundle after execution. No Validation or Final Test.

## Registered change

Keep V1.1 EMA200, Donchian20 breakout entry, Donchian10/EMA exit, completed-bar filter, long-only single-position behavior, initial capital 10000 USD, leverage 1, order-based mode, and terminal liquidation unchanged. V1.2 changes opening volume only. No new price stop is added; that belongs to V1.3.

Use platform-documented TA-Lib ATR14 on the same eligible hourly historical candles. The platform's Python package computes ATR; no custom replacement ATR implementation. Sizing is a strategy rule, not an invented platform dropdown option:

- Flat-account availableBalance supplies the sizing budget.
- Raw volume = min(0.001 × availableBalance / (2 × ATR14), 0.20 × availableBalance / current ask, 0.02 lot), for verified contractSize=1.
- Round down in model-chosen 0.01-lot increments; skip if below 0.01. This discretization is not a claim about the account's minimum volume/step. Invalid ATR or insufficient capital skips the opening order. A platform rejection is retained, not silently worked around.
- The 2×ATR denominator is a volatility scale, not an actual stop-loss distance or guaranteed maximum loss. Maximum opening size is twice the V1.1 0.01-lot baseline. Parameters are fixed before viewing results.
