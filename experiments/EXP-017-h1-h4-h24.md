## Model

At 01:00, 05:00, 09:00, 13:00, 17:00 and 21:00 UTC, the strategy queries **H1** OHLC candles for each BTC/ETH spot and futures pair independently. Every historical candle is strictly older than the execution callback time; H4 candles are **composed** from four contiguous H1 closes ending at UTC 00/04/08/12/16/20, and H24 closes at UTC midnight. Invalid OHLC and missing candles are rejected; neither forward-fill nor partial candles are permitted.

H4 log basis `b=ln(F/S)`; a 42-bar rolling robust z score (training median/MAD with sample-SD fallback) is evaluated on the last completed 4-hour bar. Mean reversion trade requires `abs(z)>=2.0`, estimated edge to the trailing median `>=0.002` and independent H24 regime that rejects daily spot log moves >10% or latest days with |log basis| >3.5%. For positive z, go long spot and short futures; for negative z, short spot and long futures. Signals are directional but **not** a guarantee of stationarity/carry profitability. OU half-life is computed diagnostically but is not a compulsory gate for the first candidate (`use_ou_gate=false`); this prevents an unverified low-N parameter from suppressing every trade. A later candidate can enable it.

Account stops: per-pair basis widening against entry by 0.012 triggers exit; complete H4 reversion `z` to ±0.4 triggers exit; 48h time exit; 12h post-close cooldown. Absolute live basis >3.5%, H1-to-snapshot basis discontinuity >2.5%, pair mismatch >1%, insufficient lot or exposure budget veto NEW entries. Aim gross notional <=15% NAV per pair (7.5% each leg), max 30% if both active. 0.01-lot quantity precision is a **hypothesis** to verify against actual platform trade records and contract rules. No statement is made that stops always execute at a bounded loss (hourly gaps may slip). Real NAV peak drawdown >8% stops all new entry and exits confirmed pairs. Orders are state tracked using native trade IDs and halt if uncertain; rescue of unconfirmed fills is not atomic.

IMPORTANT: since ALGOGENE `isSync` reflects the entire subscribed basket, a pair can be individually synchronized and processed while the other pair is stale even if `isSync=false`; this implementation checks **each pair's two timestamps**. If one pair has missing or corrupt history the other may continue.

## Interpretive limits

- Comparing one EXP017 model on 2023-2025 to another model selected after looking at this period is in-sample research; additional chronological holdouts are recommended for credibility.
- H24 is a **regime veto**, not a standalone predictive factor; H4 and OU return characteristics are hypotheses to test on the backtest.
- The two pairs may trade different underlying venues/contract expiries; product type FUT from metadata does not prove same-venue executable cash-and-carry.
- No funding curve, financing, realistic order-book slippage or intra-hour stop execution model added, so do not call it a fully specified funding-arbitrage strategy.
- The existing EXP016 2024-05 ETH outlier and the H4 2021 OHLC issue require event-level audit before any live deployment.
- The strategy's initial 440-H1 query per instrument at H4 decisions is designed for correctness and explicit missing-data detection; profile cloud runtime before considering optimization/caching.

Initial objective: improve ALGOGENE native Score_Total, especially `score_TradePerDay` and `score_AvgDrawdownDuration`, **without** sacrificing net PnL, realized Sharpe or execution integrity. Any measured improvement must quote the true platform score, with run and native Backtest IDs.
