# EXP-008 — V1.3 hourly native ATR stop

Role: Development. Requested period: 2021-01 through 2023-12. Registered result: NOT RUN. No Validation or Final Test. Original platform responses will be archived automatically after execution.

## Registered change from V1.2

V1.3 adds one protective mechanism: attach the documented native OrderObject.stopLossLevel to each market buy at current ask minus 2 × ATR14. The platform evaluates this order condition and determines the actual fill. Do not simulate stop fills, substitute stop prices for actual fills, or invent a finer execution timeframe.

Keep BTCUSD, hourly dataset=60 and interval H, EMA200, Donchian20 entry, Donchian10/EMA exit, completed-bar filter, ATR14 sizing, capital budget, 0.01-lot model rounding, maximum 0.02 lot, long-only, one position, initial capital 10000 USD, leverage 1, order-based execution, terminal liquidation, and platform cost settings identical to V1.2. The only platform settings-file change is strategyName. No trailing stop, synthetic daily-loss cutoff, alternate leverage mode, or parameter search is added to this experiment.

The stop is fixed at submission and referenced to ask, not retrospectively recalculated from a hoped-for fill. It must be finite, positive and below the entry reference; otherwise skip the order. A stop closure is handled through the existing successful close callback, which clears the held trade ID. Duplicate events cannot create duplicate exits/reentries. Existing signal exits remain active. Gaps and platform execution rules mean the stop does not guarantee a maximum realized loss.
